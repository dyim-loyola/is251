## 🧭 Overview

Create a small Airtable database using an idea that interests you. Your goal is to demonstrate that you can organize your own real data into tables and connect related records.

You may build your database from scratch or customize one of the suggested templates. If you use a template, you must modify it and replace the sample content with your own data.

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By completing this assignment, you will be able to:

- Explain the purpose of tables, fields, and records
- Organize related information into separate tables
- Select appropriate Airtable field types
- Connect tables using linked records
- Demonstrate how a relational database works

## 📚 Airtable Learning Resources

Use the [Airtable Academy: Builder Essentials](https://academy.airtable.com/path/builder-essentials) learning path as a reference.

Focus on these modules:

1. **Getting Started with App Building**  
   Learn how to build from scratch or customize an existing Airtable app.

2. **Data Layer Foundations**  
   Learn how to create and manage tables, fields, records, data, and views.

3. **Mapping Your Workflow**  
   Learn how relational databases organize information and connect related tables.

You do not need to complete the advanced modules about interfaces, automations, synchronization, or AI features.

## 🚀 Choose an Approach

You may create your own database idea or begin with one of these templates:

- 📚 [Student Study Guide Template](https://www.airtable.com/templates/study-guides/expgvEEs9ehSRRYrR)
- 💼 [Student Job Search Template](https://www.airtable.com/templates/student-job-search/expvtfBrVBr0jxcwE)

Templates are starting points only. Your submitted base must show your own independent work and contain your own real data.

Possible original ideas include:

- Courses linked to study resources
- Jobs linked to companies or applications
- Books linked to authors
- Projects linked to tasks
- Events linked to participants
- Meals linked to ingredients

## 🛠️ Airtable Requirements

Your Airtable base must include:

1. At least **two clearly named tables**
2. Appropriate fields in each table
3. Your own meaningful, real data in both tables
4. A **linked-record field** connecting the two tables
5. Enough linked records to clearly demonstrate that the relationship works
6. Descriptive names for your tables and fields

For example:

- A job application links to a company
- A study resource links to a course
- A task links to a project
- A book links to an author

Do not represent the relationship using only plain text. You must use Airtable’s **Link to another record** field type.

## 🎥 Video Demonstration

Create a **3–5 minute screen-recorded video** demonstrating your Airtable database.

In your video:

- Explain the purpose of your database
- Show your two or more tables
- Explain what a table, field, and record represent
- Identify the primary field in each table
- Show the real data you added
- Demonstrate how the linked-record field connects the tables
- Open linked records to show that the relationship works
- Explain why separating the information into related tables is useful
- Describe how you customized the base or built it independently

Submit the recording as an `.mp4` or `.webm` file, following the same submission process used for Video Reflections.
