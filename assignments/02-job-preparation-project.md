## 📝 Part 1: Resume Creation (50 Points)

Create a professional resume that showcases your qualifications and experiences.

### 📋 Requirements

- 📤 Upload your resume in Word format (.docx) - PDF format is not allowed
- 🏢 Visit [Career Services](https://www.loyola.edu/department/career-center/) for resume review and formatting guidance
  - 📝 Document your visit (date, advisor name, and summary of assistance)
- ✅ Complete the submission form with all required information

### 📏 Resume Guidelines

- 📄 Length: 1 page (2 pages maximum)
- 🔤 Format: 11-point Times New Roman font with 1-inch margins
- 📊 Content: Clear summary of qualifications, experience, skills, and interests

### 🛠️ Resources

- 📑 [Resume Templates](https://www.loyola.edu/department/career-center/resources/document-preparation/resumes.html): Available on the Resources page under "Career Topic Guides" > "Resume Development"
- 🤝 Career Help: Visit the Rizzo Career Center
  - 📅 Schedule appointments through Handshake (virtual or in-person)
  - ⏱️ Drop-in hours: Monday to Friday, 12:00 PM to 3:00 PM

---

Creating a professional resume is an essential first step in preparing for your job interview. Your resume will help you identify and articulate your skills and experiences, which you'll need to effectively communicate during the interview process. The resume you create in Part 1 will serve as the foundation for the interview preparation you'll complete in Part 2.

## 🎤 Part 2: Mock Interview Preparation (50 Points)

Practice your interview skills using the Big Interview platform.

### 📋 Requirements

- 💼 Select a job posting from [Handshake](https://loyola.joinhandshake.com/login) or other job sites that interests you
- 🎬 Complete a mock interview through the **Big Interview** platform
- 📸 Screenshot showing completion of your Big Interview session
  - 🔗 URL link to your completed Big Interview session
  - 🤖 AI evaluation of your performance (.txt or .docx)

### 🔑 How to Access Big Interview

- 🌐 Go to Big Interview ([loyola.biginterview.com](http://loyola.biginterview.com))
- 🔐 Log in with your Loyola credentials
- 🔍 Find the assignment **IS251-Job Interview-FA26**
- 🔢 Code will be provided by your instructor - see the Assignment page in Moodle for details

## 🎥 Part 3: Job-Specific Interview (100 Points)

Create and record your own video responding to job-specific interview questions.

### 📋 Requirements

- 💼 Select a job posting from your career search that interests you
- 📝 Generate 3-5 technical or job-specific questions based on this posting using AI:
  - 📱 Copy the job description and use an AI tool like ChatGPT
  - 🤖 Use this prompt: *"Based on this job description, generate 3-5 technical interview questions that assess the specific skills required for this position. Include questions that test both theoretical knowledge and practical experience. For each question, also explain what skill or knowledge area it's testing."*
- 🎥 Record yourself answering these questions (3-5 minutes)
