# Attendance and Participation

- **Evaluated:** Throughout the semester
- **Total Points:** 200

## 🔍 Overview

Attendance and Participation is an ongoing course assignment and grading component that recognizes how you prepare for class and contribute to collaborative learning. Unlike a single submission, your score develops across the semester through pre-class Loom annotations, in-class Miro work, and active involvement in class activities.

The purpose of this component is to encourage consistent preparation, thoughtful engagement, and constructive contributions to your own learning and the learning of your classmates. Multiple participation styles are valued. You may demonstrate engagement by speaking up, asking useful questions, contributing to group work, helping classmates, completing an assigned survey or poll, participating in demonstrations, or engaging in another course activity. Simply being present does not count as participation.

## ✅ How Participation Is Assessed

### 🎥 Pre-Class Loom Annotations — 50 Points

Complete assigned Loom video annotations before class. Annotations should demonstrate relevant and substantive preparation rather than simple acknowledgement. Points are calculated proportionally:

**50 × (completed assigned annotations ÷ assigned annotations)**

### 🧩 In-Class Miro Activity — 50 Points

Contribute meaningful individual work to assigned Miro boards during class. Activity counts provide evidence of participation, while the relevance and substance of contributions also matter. Points are calculated proportionally:

**50 × (completed assigned activities ÷ assigned activities)**

### 🤝 In-Class Participation — 100 Points

In-class participation includes speaking up, asking questions, contributing to discussions and group work, supporting peers, participating in demonstrations, and completing surveys or polls when they are assigned. Surveys are one form of participation evidence and may not be administered in every module.

Participation is evaluated across the semester using the full range of available evidence:

| Engagement Level | Description | Points |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Consistent | Regularly prepared and makes constructive contributions across class activities | 100 |
| Regular | Participates meaningfully and reliably, with some inconsistency | 80 |
| Occasional | Contributes at times but engagement is uneven | 60 |
| Limited | Demonstrates minimal involvement in class activities | 20 |
| Not demonstrated | No meaningful evidence of participation | 0 |

## 📊 Grading Rubric

| Component | Evidence | Points |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Pre-class Loom annotations | Completed annotations showing relevant, substantive preparation before class | 50 |
| In-class Miro activity | Individual activity count and meaningful contributions on assigned boards | 50 |
| In-class participation | Constructive involvement through discussion, questions, group work, peer support, demonstrations, surveys or polls, and other activities | 100 |
| **Total** |  | **200** |

## ⚠️ Attendance Policy

Attendance does not earn points by itself. However, **six or more absences result in a 100-point deduction from the earned Attendance and Participation score**. The sixth absence triggers the deduction, and the final score cannot be lower than zero. University-approved accommodations and documented circumstances are handled according to applicable university policies.

## 🎯 Final Note

Participation is evaluated over time rather than through a single performance. Focus on preparing before class, contributing meaningfully during class, and supporting a respectful and collaborative learning environment.
