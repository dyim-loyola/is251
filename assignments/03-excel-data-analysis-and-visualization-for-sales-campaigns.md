## 🛠 Tasks

### 1️⃣ Data Analysis

- 📦 **Calculate Total Units Sold** for:

  - 👗 Women’s Clothing *(pre-calculated)*
  - 👠 Women’s Shoes
  - 👔 Men’s Clothing
  - 👞 Men’s Shoes

    Include: **Total**, **Largest**, and **Smallest** Units Sold.
- 📊 **Average Sales** for each season (Men’s & Women’s products).

  Determine which product sold more in each category. *(Spring averages pre-calculated)*

💡 **Tips:**

- Copy formulas for efficiency:
  - D25:F27 → from C25:C27
  - G3:I22 → from G2:I2

📋 **Questions:**

1. Which category had:
   - 🥇 Highest total units sold?
   - 📈 Largest number of units sold?
   - 📉 Smallest number of units sold?
2. Which category had the **best gross profit**?

---

### 2️⃣ Create a Graph

- ➕ Add a **new worksheet** named **Spring Campaigns**.
- 📋 Copy **Product Sales Data** for:
  - Quarter, Month-Year, and all 4 product categories.
- 📈 Create a **line graph** of **Spring sales revenue**.

✅ **Graph must include:**

- Labeled axes & legend
- Title: *"Spring Sales Data"*
- Data table visible & formatted

💡 **Hint:** Use filters to show only **Spring** data.

📋 **Questions:**

1. Will sales be higher in 2020 than 2018? Justify with trends.
2. Which category will likely perform best in 2018?

---

### 3️⃣ Generate Sales Rank

- 🔍 Use **VLOOKUP** to assign Men’s & Women’s **Average Sales Rank**.
- Ranking scale:
  - 0–100 → Low
  - 100–150 → Normal
  - 150–250 → High
  - 250 → Exceptional

📋 **Questions:**

1. Who performed better: Men’s or Women’s?
2. How many seasons did Men’s rank “Exceptional”? *(Use `COUNTIF`)*

---

### 4️⃣ Create a Pivot Table

- ➕ Add **Season Total** column.
- 📊 Pivot Table setup:
  - **Rows:** Year
  - **Columns:** Quarter
  - **Values:** Season Total

📋 **Questions:**

1. Which season had:
   - 🏆 Highest **overall** sales?
   - 📊 Highest **average** sales?
2. Which year had the most seasonal variation?

💡 **Tip:** Adjust Pivot Table settings for accuracy.
