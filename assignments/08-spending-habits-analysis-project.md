## Description

## **Snapshot**

150 points   
Due by Sunday, 11:59 PM ET  
 **Introduction**: You are evaluating a number of rewards credit card to get the best value. To do so, you will gather your past spending behavior, analyze your habits, and choose the best credit card(s) by forecasting your modified spending behavior.

**Learning objective**: develop financial literacy skills by analyzing your past transactions and understand the importance of personal finance management.

**Requirements:**

- Your spending data must cover at least three *consecutive*months or more. You can track individual transaction manually or access electronic statements through your credit card provider's online portal or mobile app.
  - The sampling period must include at least two months of the semester.
- Your spending data must have at least **100 transaction or more**
- Your spending data must have at least five or more spending categories
- Your analysis should include at least five or more Charts or Tables
  - Must include monthly summary of your expenditure using Pivot Table
- Your comparison of rewards credit card should be at least three or more cards using VLOOKUP

## **Guidance**

Your submission should include (1) an Excel file showing your data, analysis, and findings, (2) your summary report by answering the questions in the form, and (3) a 3–5 minute screen-recorded video demonstration.

**Instructions:**

1. Create a spreadsheet or use a database to record and categorize **each** transaction from your past purchase transactions.
2. **Categorize** transactions into broad categories such as "Essential Expenses" (e.g., groceries, utilities), "Discretionary Expenses" (e.g., dining out, entertainment), "Bills & Subscriptions" (e.g., cable, streaming services), "Transportation" (e.g., fuel, public transportation), or any other applicable categories.
3. Using a **Pivot Table**, calculate the total amount spent in (1) **each category** for (2) **each month**. (Hint: use **both** rows and columns of Pivot Table)
4. Using **Graphs,** analyze the monthly expenditure patterns by comparing the category-wise spending for each month. (Hint: **PivotChart** or **Analyze** feature)
5. Identify any significant changes or trends in your spending habits over the analyzed period.
6. Search (e.g. [NerdWallet](https://www.nerdwallet.com/credit-cards/best/cash-back?msockid=2de9e2b1e15661fd25def68ae094606d)) and **select at least 3 or more** cash rewards cards that offer varying cash rewards rate for different categories
7. Analyze which rewards card(s) will generate most savings of your future spending habits. (Hint: Use **VLOOKUP** for cash rewards rates)
8. Finalize your analysis and present a short summary of your findings, including how you might (or not) change your spending habits.

**Submission:**

You will write a summary report of what have learned from analyzing your spending habits.

- Discuss any changes you plan to make to your spending habits or budget as a result of this analysis. Ensure that your analysis is well-organized, clearly presented, and supported by relevant findings.
- Your deliverables should include the Excel workbook, completed Microsoft Form, and a 3–5 minute video recording (.mp4 or .webm).
- **Note:** If you are submitting credit card statements as part of your data, remember to handle your personal financial information with care and ensure the privacy and security of your credit card statements. For example, you can change the name of the vendor such as Costco to a generic same such as Warehouse Retail; however, your data must be real transactions. You can choose to remove transactions data you do not wish to share, but your overall data must include real transactions in sufficient quantity to conduct a trend analysis.
