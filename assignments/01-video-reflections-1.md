# Video Reflections 1

- **Due:** Saturday, 12:00 AM
- **Total Points:** 50


> 🎥 **Recording your video:** Use Microsoft Clipchamp to record your camera, microphone, and screen, then export the completed recording as an `.mp4` file. Follow the [Microsoft Clipchamp Recording Guide](https://is251-course-guides.dobinyim.chatgpt.site/clipchamp-recording) before submitting in Moodle.

## Description

## 🔍 Overview

Review what you have learned from **all course modules completed through the current week**. Your goal is to explain how your thinking has developed through course concepts, class activities, and interactions with classmates—not simply summarize the modules.

## 🎥 Screen-Recorded Reflection (1–3 minutes)

Use your screen recording to **explain your learning and show evidence of your participation**.

In your recording:

- 📘 Explain one course concept that reinforced, challenged, or changed your thinking.
- 🖥️ Show one contribution you made to a class activity. Examples include a **Loom video annotation**, your **Miro sticky notes**, or your contribution to an **in-group discussion**.
- 👥 Describe one idea from a classmate that helped you understand the topic differently or more clearly.
- 🔄 Connect these experiences to how your understanding has developed across the modules completed so far.

If your contribution was part of a discussion without a visual artifact, briefly explain what you contributed and show the related course material. Do not display private information about classmates.

## 📌 Submission Guidelines

- **Format:** Only `.mp4` or `.webm`
- **Length:** 1–3 minutes
- **Deadline:** Submit by **Saturday at 12:00 AM**
- **Naming:** `LastName_FirstName_Week#.mp4` or `.webm`

## 🤝 Participation & Engagement

Participation is required for full credit and includes attendance, completion of in-class surveys or polls, contributions to Miro or other activities, and constructive participation in group or class discussions. Multiple participation styles are valued.

## 📊 Grading Breakdown (50 Points)

| Component | Evidence | Points |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Course learning | Explains how learning changed across modules | 20 |
| Contribution evidence | Shows and explains a Loom annotation, Miro post, group contribution, or similar activity | 20 |
| Learning from others | Identifies and explains a helpful peer contribution | 10 |

## 🎯 Final Note

Focus on what you **contributed, learned, and reconsidered**. Clear evidence and thoughtful reflection matter more than production quality.
