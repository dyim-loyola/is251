# Module 12: Systems Analysis and Design

## 📝 Module Overview

This shortened module introduces students to the **foundations of systems analysis and design**, focusing on how IT solutions are developed to solve real business problems. Instead of extended case studies, students will engage in **hands-on demos and group practice** using modern tools (Miro and AI coding assistants) to get a feel for design thinking and prototyping.

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, students will be able to:

1. **Explain** the purpose of systems analysis and design in solving business problems.
2. **Apply** basic design techniques using Miro’s wireframe and prototype tools.
3. **Experiment** with AI-powered “vibecoding” to assist in simple system or UI design.
4. **Collaborate** in groups to practice design skills and share feedback.

---

## 🔑 Key Concepts

- **Systems Analysis** – identifying business problems and IT solution requirements.
- **Human-Centered Design** – focusing on user needs when building solutions.
- **Wireframes & Prototypes** – visual representations of systems and interfaces.
- **AI-Assisted Design (Vibecoding)** – using generative AI tools to brainstorm or produce code/design ideas quickly.

---

## 📚 Pre-Class Preparation

- Read **Chapter 13 - Acquiring Information Systems and Applications**: Covers IT planning, acquisition strategies, systems development life cycle, and vendor selection.

---

## 📝 Assessment

- **Participation**: Hands-on activity in both sessions.
- **Exit Ticket**: Quick reflection on how design tools (Miro/AI) can help create better business systems.
