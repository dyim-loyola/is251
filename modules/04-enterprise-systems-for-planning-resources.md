# Module 04: Enterprise Systems for Planning Resources

## 📋 Module Overview

This session introduces **Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) systems** and how they help organizations manage people, money, and materials more efficiently. Students will explore real-world examples, see how ERP integrates different business areas, and understand why it’s a game-changer for decision-making.

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this session, students will be able to:

1. **💡 Explain** the role of Enterprise Systems in resource planning.
2. **🔍 Identify** how technology enhances financial and human capital management.
3. **📊 Describe** how forecasting techniques improve resource decisions.
4. **🏆 Recognize** competitive advantages from successful ERP implementation.

---

## 📘 Key Concepts and Terms

- **🏢 Enterprise Systems (ERP)** – Integrated software that connects business functions like finance, HR, and supply chain into one system.
- **📅 Resource Planning** – Making sure people, money, and materials are used efficiently.
- **👩‍💼 Human Capital** – Skills, knowledge, and abilities employees bring to an organization.
- **💰 Financial Capital** – Money and other financial resources used to run a business.
- **📈 Forecasting** – Using past data to predict future needs.

---

## 💼 Practical Applications

- **SAP & Oracle ERP** – Used by global companies to connect finance, HR, and operations.
- **Case Study:** A manufacturer cut costs by 20% by using ERP for better inventory forecasting.
- **Forecasting Tools:** Time series analysis, regression, and even machine learning to plan ahead.
