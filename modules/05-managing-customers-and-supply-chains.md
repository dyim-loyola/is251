# Module 6: Managing Customers and Supply Chain

## 🔍 Module Overview

This session introduces how Information Technology supports both **Customer Relationship Management (CRM)** and **Supply Chain Management (SCM)**.

Students will explore how these systems connect, how they improve customer experiences, and how they help businesses work better with suppliers.

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this session, students will be able to:

1. **🔄 Explain** the stages of the customer lifecycle and their importance.
2. **💬 Describe** how CRM systems help manage customer relationships.
3. **🔗 Explain** how SCM improves supplier coordination and efficiency.
4. **🧠 Analyze** how CRM and SCM work together to improve business operations.

---

## 📘 Key Concepts

- **👤 Customer Lifecycle** – Stages a customer goes through, from awareness to loyalty.
- **🤝 CRM (Customer Relationship Management)** – Software and strategies to improve customer interactions and satisfaction.
- **🏭 SCM (Supply Chain Management)** – Managing suppliers, manufacturing, and distribution for efficiency.
- **🌐 Enterprise Systems** – Integrated systems that connect business functions like sales, logistics, and HR.
