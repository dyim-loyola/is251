# Module 01: Introduction to Information Systems

## 📋 Module Overview

This session introduces the fundamentals of Information Systems (IS) and their role in modern organizations. Students will connect theory to practice, see how IS supports decision-making, and learn how to use course-related tools for assignments and video submissions.

**⏱️ Total Duration:** 75 minutes (single session)

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this session, students will be able to:

1. **🔍 Define** key components and functions of Information Systems.
2. **🔄 Explain** the relationship between IS and organizational effectiveness.
3. **💬 Discuss** the role of technology adoption, including benefits and challenges.
4. **🎥 Record, export, and submit** a video assignment using Microsoft Clipchamp.
5. **🤔 Reflect** on the role of IT in daily life and business.

---

## 📘 Key Concepts and Terms

- **💻 Information System (IS)** – A coordinated set of components for collecting, storing, and delivering information and digital products.
- **🖥️ Information Technology (IT)** – Technology used to store, retrieve, and communicate information.
- **🧠 Decision-Making** – Choosing actions based on data and analysis.
- **📈 Technology Adoption Models** – Frameworks explaining how new technology is embraced.
- **📹 Microsoft Clipchamp** – Tool for recording your camera, microphone, and screen and exporting an MP4 assignment file
