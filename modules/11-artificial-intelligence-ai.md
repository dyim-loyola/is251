# Module 11: Artificial Intelligence AI

## 🤖 Overview

This module introduces **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** applications in business, focusing on **hands-on exploration** of AI services for vision, speech, text, video, and emotion. Students will experiment with industry tools like AWS AI services, Tavus, Hume, and ChatGPT. The emphasis is on *experience and reflection* rather than deep theory.

**Watch the module introduction video in Moodle.**

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, students will be able to:

1. **Explain** what AI is in simple terms and identify its main categories.
2. **Try out** AI services for vision, speech, and text analysis using AWS Educate.
3. **Experiment with** AI tools for video generation, audio/emotion recognition, and conversation.
4. **Reflect** on how AI tools could transform business processes and customer experiences.
5. **Discuss** ethical considerations around AI use in business.

---

## 🔑 Key Concepts

- **Discriminative AI** – AI that classifies or identifies things (e.g., text, images).
- **Generative AI** – AI that creates new content (e.g., text, images, videos).
- **AI in Business** – Improving efficiency, enhancing customer experiences, and enabling new products.
- **Ethical Considerations** – Privacy, bias, transparency, and responsible use.
