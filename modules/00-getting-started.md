# 🚀 Getting Started with IS251

Welcome to **IS251: Data Analytics and Information Systems**!

I’m Dobin Yim, a faculty member in the Information Systems, Law, and Operations Management Department at Loyola University Maryland. My teaching and industry experience includes AI, cloud computing, big data, data science, and helping organizations use information technology to improve business processes. You can learn more through my [LinkedIn profile](https://www.linkedin.com/in/dobin-yim-53026b31) and [research publications](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-9360-8343).

In this course, you will learn how organizations use information technology and data to improve business processes and make better decisions. You will also develop practical experience with tools such as Excel, Airtable, Tableau, Miro, Microsoft Clipchamp, Loom, and Microsoft Copilot.

## 📋 About This Course

**Watch the course introduction video in Moodle.**

## 🧭 Where to Find Everything

This course uses a course website and Moodle for different purposes.

| Platform | What You Will Use It For |
|---|---|
| **Course Website** | Weekly modules, learning objectives, readings, key concepts, assignment instructions, examples, and helpful resources |
| **Moodle** | Announcements, Loom video links, Miro board links, assignment submission areas, due dates, feedback, and grades |
| **Miro** | Collaborative class activities, group discussions, diagrams, and sticky-note contributions |
| **Loom** | Instructor demonstration videos linked through Moodle |
| **Microsoft Clipchamp** | Student screen-and-camera recordings exported as MP4 files |
| **Microsoft 365** | Excel assignments, forms, documents, and other course files |

> **Learn on the course website. Use Moodle to access course tools and submit work.**

The **course website is the primary source for instructional content and assignment directions**. Moodle provides access to activities and submission areas. Always confirm the official due date and your submission status in Moodle.

## 🔄 Recommended Weekly Workflow

For each module:

1. Open the module on the **course website**.
2. Review the learning objectives, key concepts, readings, and assignment instructions.
3. Open Moodle to access any linked **Loom videos** or **Miro boards**.
4. Participate in the required Miro or in-class activities.
5. Complete the assignment using the instructions on the course website.
6. Return to Moodle and upload every required deliverable.
7. Confirm that Moodle shows the assignment as **Submitted**, not Draft.

## ✅ First-Week Checklist

Complete these steps before beginning the first module:

- Open and bookmark the **IS251 course website**.
- Confirm that you can access the IS251 Moodle course.
- Review the syllabus and course policies.
- Join the course Miro team using your Loyola email address.
- Change your Miro profile name to your full name.
- Confirm that you can open an instructor Loom video.
- Review the Moodle assignment-submission instructions.
- Complete the learning-preferences survey.
- Contact the instructor if you cannot access a required platform.

## 🎨 Getting Started with Miro

We will use Miro for collaborative activities, group discussions, and visual contributions.

Before the first activity:

- Accept the course Miro invitation.
- Use your Loyola email address.
- Use your full name as it appears on the class roster.
- Allow 24–48 hours for access to the course space.
- Practice creating a sticky note, zooming, and moving around a board.

[Open the complete Miro setup guide](https://is251-course-guides.dobinyim.chatgpt.site/miro-setup).

Your own Miro sticky notes and contributions may also serve as evidence of class participation or reflection.

## 📤 Submitting Assignments in Moodle

Before submitting:

- Read the complete instructions on the course website.
- Check the required file types.
- Confirm that every required file, link, or video is included.
- Upload your work before the Moodle deadline.
- Allow extra time for large video files or connection problems.

After uploading, confirm that Moodle displays **Submitted**. A submission showing **Draft** may not be considered complete.

If you submit the wrong file, contact the instructor as soon as possible. Reopening a submitted assignment may take time.

[Review the complete Moodle submission guide](https://is251-course-guides.dobinyim.chatgpt.site/moodle-submission).

## 🎥 Instructor Videos and Student Recordings

Instructor demonstration videos may be provided through Loom links in Moodle. When an assignment requires your own recording, use Microsoft Clipchamp and follow the [Microsoft Clipchamp Recording Guide](https://is251-course-guides.dobinyim.chatgpt.site/clipchamp-recording).

When an assignment requires your own video:

- Follow the required time limit.
- Record your screen while showing your work.
- Explain what you did in your own words.
- Protect private or sensitive information.
- Export the completed Clipchamp recording as an `.mp4` file.
- Submit the required video file through Moodle.
- Test the file or sharing permissions before the deadline.

## 📊 Grades and Feedback

Use Moodle to review assignment status, instructor feedback, and grades. When checking your progress during the semester, focus on the points earned and read the feedback for each assignment.

## 🆘 Where to Get Help

- **Course content or assignment question:** Contact the instructor.
- **Moodle access or submission problem:** Contact Loyola technical support and notify the instructor.
- **Miro access problem:** Confirm that you joined with your Loyola email and allow 24–48 hours.
- **Broken website or video link:** Send the instructor the module name and a screenshot of the problem.

## 🎯 Getting Started Learning Objectives

After completing this orientation, you should be able to:

1. Explain the different purposes of the course website and Moodle.
2. Locate weekly content and assignment instructions on the course website.
3. Access Loom videos and Miro activities through Moodle.
4. Submit assignments correctly and confirm their submission status.
5. Identify where to get help with course tools.

Finally, complete this **learning-preferences survey — available in Moodle** to share any learning preferences or special needs.
