# Module 7: Social Computing

## 📘 Overview

This session explores how social computing shapes modern business and society. Students will examine how organizations use social media and online communities to understand customers, improve products, and predict trends. Through interactive discussions and group activities, students will connect theory to practice while considering the ethical implications of social technologies.

**Watch the module introduction video in Moodle.**

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this session, students will be able to:

1. **Define** social computing and related concepts.
2. **Analyze** how organizations use social media for product development and forecasting.
3. **Evaluate** the ethical challenges of social data use.
4. **Apply** critical thinking to real-world social computing examples.

---

## 📌 Key Concepts

- **Social Computing** – use of digital platforms to enhance interaction and gather insights.
- **Social Listening** – monitoring online conversations for customer feedback.
- **Online Communities** – digital spaces for collaboration and shared interests.

## 📚 Pre-Class Activities

- **Textbook reading — Chapter 9: Social Computing:** Explore social media, Web 2.0 technologies, and problems such as bots, fake news, and deepfakes.
- **Prepare for Assignment 3 — Build a Linked Airtable Database:** Use the [Airtable Academy: Builder Essentials](https://academy.airtable.com/path/builder-essentials) learning path. Focus on these modules:

  1. **Getting Started with App Building** — learn how to create an Airtable app from scratch or customize a template.
  2. **Data Layer Foundations** — learn how to create tables, fields, records, field types, and views.
  3. **Mapping Your Workflow** — learn how relational databases separate related information and connect tables.

After completing these modules, you should be ready to:

- Create at least **two clearly named tables**.
- Add appropriate fields and choose suitable field types.
- Enter your own meaningful, real data in both tables.
- Create a **Link to another record** field that connects the tables.
- Open linked records and explain how the relationship works.

You do not need the advanced Builder Essentials modules on interfaces, automations, synchronization, or AI features for Assignment 3.
