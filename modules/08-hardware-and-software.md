# Module 08: Hardware and Software

## 🌐 Overview

This session introduces students to the **foundations of hardware and software** as the building blocks of Business Information Systems. Students will examine the core components of their own devices, explore the relationship between hardware and software, and discuss the role of software in shaping modern business, using the article *“Developers are the New Bankers.”*

**Watch the module introduction video in Moodle.**

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this session, students will be able to:

1. **Identify** their own device’s CPU, GPU, RAM, storage, and operating system.
2. **Differentiate** between hardware and software in simple business contexts.
3. **Explain** how hardware and software depend on each other.
4. **Evaluate** the impact of software on business through discussion of the assigned article.

---

## 📌 Key Concepts

- **Hardware** – Physical devices (CPU, RAM, GPU, storage).
- **Software** – Operating systems and applications.
- **Layered Architecture** – Hardware → OS → Applications.
- **Interdependence** – Hardware enables processing; software provides instructions.
- **Business Relevance** – Developers as drivers of innovation in industries.

- **Read:** Textbook Technical Guide Chapter 2 - "Software Systems and Applications" covering operating systems and business applications

## 🗂️ Case/Application Examples

- **Student Hardware Look-Up**: Students check their own CPU, GPU, RAM, storage space, and OS.
- **Software Case Discussion**: *“Developers are the New Bankers”* → how software drives business models and strategy.

## 📝 Assessment

- **In-class activity**: Identify personal device hardware specs and OS.
- **Exit Ticket**: *“What’s one way hardware limits or enables the software you use daily?”*
