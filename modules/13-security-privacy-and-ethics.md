# Module 13: Security Privacy and Ethics

## 📝Overview

This final module explores **security, privacy, and ethics** in information systems. Instead of lecture-heavy content, students will engage in **discussions, debates, and case-based activities** to think critically about the impact of technology on individuals, organizations, and society. We will also conclude the course with a **15-minute course summary and project reflections**.

**Watch the module introduction video in Moodle.**

---

## 🎯Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, students will be able to:

1. **Discuss** the meaning of ethics and how it applies to technology use.
2. **Analyze** real-world dilemmas around security, privacy, and surveillance.
3. **Examine** how AI bias shows up in the workplace and its implications.
4. **Evaluate** threats to information systems (external and internal).
5. **Reflect** on their overall learning journey in the course.

---

## 🔑 Key Concepts

- **Ethics & Responsibility**: How organizations and individuals decide what’s “right.”
- **Privacy**: Data rights, surveillance technologies, and trust.
- **AI Bias**: When systems make unfair decisions, and the workplace impact.
- **Security**: Protecting systems against external threats (e.g., IoMT) and internal threats (e.g., social engineering).
