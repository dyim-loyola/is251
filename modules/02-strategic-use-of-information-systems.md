# Module 02: Strategic Use of Information Systems

## 📋 Module Overview

In this module, you will learn how businesses compete, how to analyze their market position, and how information technology (IT) can help them gain and sustain a competitive advantage. You will apply three key frameworks — Porter's Five Forces, the Value Chain, and STP (Segmentation, Targeting, Positioning) — to a real-world example. Working in teams, you will create a product positioning map in **Miro** and discuss how IT can improve key business processes to strengthen your competitive position.

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this session, you will be able to:

1. 🔍 Explain the role of Porter's Five Forces, the Value Chain, and STP in shaping business strategy.
2. 📊 Create a product positioning map to visualize competition.
3. ⚙️ Identify key primary and secondary business processes.
4. 💡 Propose IT-based improvements to support strategic advantage.

## 📘 Key Concepts and Terms

- **🏭 Porter’s Five Forces** – Framework for analyzing industry competition through suppliers, buyers, substitutes, new entrants, and rivalry.
- **🔗 Value Chain** – Model for identifying primary and secondary activities that create value for customers.
- **🎯 STP (Segmentation, Targeting, Positioning)** – Marketing approach to focus resources on the right customer segments.
- **📊 Product Positioning Map** – Visual tool comparing competitors on selected attributes.
- **⚙️ Primary Activities** – Core business functions like inbound logistics, operations, marketing, sales, and service.
- **🔧 Secondary (Support) Activities** – Support functions such as procurement, HR, technology, and infrastructure.
- **💻 IT-Enabled Process Improvement** – Use of technology to enhance efficiency, accuracy, or customer experience.
