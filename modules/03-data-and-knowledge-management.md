# Module 03: Data and Knowledge Management

## 📋 Module Overview

In this session, you will learn the difference between **data**, **information**, and **knowledge**, and how businesses use them to make better decisions. We’ll explore the **DIKW Pyramid** (Data → Information → Knowledge → Wisdom) and discuss why capturing knowledge can give a company an edge over competitors. You’ll also try a **simple relationship mapping activity** to see how data connects in a business context.

**Watch the module introduction video in Moodle.**

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this session, you will be able to:

1. Define and distinguish between **data**, **information**, and **knowledge**.
2. Explain the purpose of the **DIKW Pyramid**.
3. Identify why **tacit knowledge** is harder to capture than explicit knowledge.
4. Map simple relationships between data elements in a business example.

---

## 📘 Key Concepts and Terms

- **📊 Data** – Raw facts and figures (e.g., “100 sales last week”).
- **📄 Information** – Organized data with meaning (e.g., “Sales increased 10% from last week”).
- **🧠 Knowledge** – Insights gained from information (e.g., “Sales rise when promotions run on weekends”).
- **📚 Tacit Knowledge** – Know-how that’s personal and hard to document (e.g., sales techniques).
- **📑 Explicit Knowledge** – Knowledge that’s written down or stored (e.g., product manuals).
- **📈 DIKW Pyramid** – Framework showing how raw data becomes wisdom for decision-making.

## 🛠 In-Class Activity – “Map It Out” Relationship Mapping

You’ll work in groups to:

1. Choose a simple business scenario (provided in class).
2. Identify **3–4 entities** (e.g., Customer, Order, Product).
3. Draw simple connections between them (e.g., Customer places Order; Order contains Product).
4. Discuss which knowledge is **explicit** and which is **tacit**.
