# Module 8: Business Analytics

## 🌐 Overview

This session introduces the fundamentals of **Business Analytics** and demonstrates its role in improving business decision-making. Students will explore the **analytics spectrum** (descriptive → predictive → prescriptive) and apply their understanding to a real-world case: Target’s use of predictive analytics.

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this session, students will be able to:

1. **Define** business analytics and explain how it enhances decision-making.
2. **Differentiate** descriptive, predictive, and prescriptive analytics with simple examples.
3. **Analyze** the Target case study to identify how predictive analytics shapes retail strategy.
4. **Discuss** the ethical concerns of using personal data in predictive modeling.

---

## 📌 Key Concepts

- **Business Analytics (BA)** – Using data, statistical methods, and models to inform decisions.
- **Descriptive Analytics** – Summarizing past data (e.g., “last quarter’s sales”).
- **Predictive Analytics** – Using past data to forecast outcomes (e.g., “who is likely to buy next?”).
- **Prescriptive Analytics** – Recommending actions to achieve desired outcomes.
- **Ethical Data Use** – Responsible handling of personal and behavioral data.
