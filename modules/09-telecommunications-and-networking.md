# Module 10: Telecommunications and Networks

## 🌐 Overview

This module introduces the role of **telecommunications and networking** in enabling modern business. Students will explore how data moves across systems, why speed and reliability matter, and how networking policies impact organizations and consumers. Real-world cases such as **net neutrality debates** and **data integrity challenges** will ground abstract concepts in practical business scenarios.

**Watch the module introduction video in Moodle.**

---

## 🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, students will be able to:

1. **Define** telecommunications and networking in simple terms.
2. **Differentiate** between bandwidth and latency using real-world examples.
3. **Explain** why data integrity and privacy are critical for business.
4. **Discuss** the implications of policies like net neutrality.
5. **Reflect** on how networking affects their personal and professional lives.

---

## 📌 Key Concepts

- **Telecommunications** – Sending information electronically over distance.
- **Networks** – Devices and systems linked to share resources and information.
- **Bandwidth vs. Latency** – “How much can flow” vs. “how quickly it starts.”
- **Data Integrity & Privacy** – Accuracy, reliability, and security of transmitted data.
- **Net Neutrality** – Debate over whether internet providers should treat all data equally.

---

## 🗂️ Case/Application Examples

- **Net Neutrality Debate** – How policy affects businesses, startups, and consumers.
- **Everyday Networking** – Zoom calls, streaming, and online gaming experiences.
