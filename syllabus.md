# Welcome!

Welcome to **IS 251: Data Analytics and Information Systems**. This course examines how organizations use information systems, data, and emerging technologies—including AI—to make better business decisions.

## Attendance and participation rubric

Attendance and participation are worth **200 points**. The points are earned through participation; attendance does not earn points by itself. Attendance is monitored for the absence penalty described below.

### Attendance — 0 points

- Attendance does not add points to the category.
- **Six or more absences result in a 100-point deduction from the student’s earned participation score.** The sixth absence triggers the deduction.
- The final Attendance and Participation score cannot be lower than zero.
- University-approved accommodations and documented circumstances are handled according to applicable university policies.

### Participation — 200 points

| Component | Evidence | Points |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Pre-class Loom annotations | Completion of assigned video annotations that show relevant, substantive preparation before class | 50 |
| In-class Miro activity | Individual activity count and meaningful contributions on assigned class boards during class | 50 |
| In-class participation | Constructive engagement through speaking up, asking questions, group work, peer support, demonstrations, assigned surveys or polls, and other class activities | 100 |
| **Total participation** |  | **200** |

For Loom and Miro, points are calculated proportionally: **50 × (completed activities ÷ assigned activities)**. In-class participation is evaluated across the semester using the full range of available evidence. Consistent constructive engagement earns 100 points; regular engagement earns 80; occasional engagement earns 60; limited engagement earns 20; and no demonstrated engagement earns 0. Surveys and polls are included when assigned, but they are not necessarily administered in every module. Multiple participation styles are valued, but simply being present does not count as participation.

## Expectations that matter most

- **Come prepared and participate.** Class sessions use Miro, Loom, surveys, discussions, demonstrations, and group activities. Multiple participation styles are valued.
- **Attendance affects your grade.** Attendance does not earn points by itself, but six or more absences result in a 100-point deduction from the Attendance and Participation score. University-approved accommodations and documented circumstances are handled under applicable university policies.
- **Submit work on time.** Assignments are normally due Saturday at 12:00 a.m. ET. The Spending Habits Analysis Project is due on your section’s scheduled final-exam date.
- **Late work is generally not accepted.** Exceptions may be approved for documented university circumstances or other extenuating circumstances. When approved, work is ordinarily due within one week of the original deadline. In-class participation activities generally cannot be made up.
- **Use AI responsibly.** When an assignment permits AI support, verify the output, protect private information, understand your own analysis, and disclose assistance when requested. Unauthorized AI-generated work may violate the Loyola Honor Code.
- **Submit through the required channel.** Deliverables must be submitted in Moodle and/or the Microsoft Form identified in the assignment. If you cannot access Moodle, notify the instructor at least 24 hours before the deadline.
